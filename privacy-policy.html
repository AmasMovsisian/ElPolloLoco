Privacy Policy

This game is a purely client-side project and does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. No tracking tools, analytics services, cookies, or external data processing are used.

All game progress, settings, and interactions remain solely on your device and are not shared with any server or third party. The game does not require registration or login.

If you access external links within the game, those websites may apply their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing the privacy information on external sites before providing any personal data.

If you have questions about this project or its functionality, feel free to contact the developer.
